Generated by tools/build_round3.py. Function: tools/muscle_deepdive.py `deepdive(days, level, baseline=None, goals=None, profile=None)` -> `{muscle_id: payload}` for all 19 muscles. Defaults: goals [muscle_size] (Size), gym profile commercial. Fixture: data/muscle-deepdive-reference.json (cases with their exact inputs, for parity with the Builder's logic/ port). ## Parity

- Feed `cases[k].inputs` (days, level, baseline null, goals, commercial profile) to the port and compare every field. Rounding: Python `round(x, n)` (half to even on the binary value) at the decimals above; compare numbers with abs tolerance 1e-4.
- Order matters for contributors, ranked_adds.items, apply and frequency.days.
- Cases: `upper-lower/intermediate/4`, `full-body/beginner/3`, `ppl/advanced/6`, `bro-split/intermediate/5` (`upper-lower/intermediate/4` is the Size / intermediate / commercial gym default the storyboard loads).
